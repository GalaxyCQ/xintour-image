01 01 · Brand Origin

一个用二十年，
经营出来的信任记号。

A Mark of Trust
Cultivated Over Twenty Years.

"等你回来，我们继续合作。"——2005年，客户的一句信任嘱托，让孕期的陈霞创办了欣途。

"We'll be waiting — let's keep working together." — A client's trust in 2005 inspired Chen Xia to found Xintour.

她没有关系背景，做事甚至有些"轴"：因风险不可控直接拒绝政府大团；为保全客人体验倒贴万元换车。二十年来，欣途见证了旅游业的跌宕起伏，却始终克制规模，选择做难而正确的事。所有的底气都来源于客人行程结束后的那一句“下次还找你们”。

No connections, just an unwavering commitment. Refusing lucrative but unsafe massive tours, taking personal losses to ensure guest comfort. Over two decades, we've witnessed the industry's wild growth but chose to stay intentionally small—taking the harder, correct path. Our confidence comes entirely from guests saying, "We'll choose you again."

"欣途从来不是一个被'打造'出来的品牌。它是一个人一点一滴经营出来的稳固大后方。"

"Xintour was never a crafted brand. It's a solid rear-guard cultivated piece by piece."

一个孕妇，在2005年的重庆，因为客户的一句"我们等你"，成立了一家小小的旅行公司。二十年间经历了三次重大转型，但有件事从来没变过：对得起每一个信任你的人。

A pregnant woman, in 2005 Chongqing, started a small travel company because a client said "we'll wait." Twenty years, three transformations. One constant: honoring every person who trusted us.
